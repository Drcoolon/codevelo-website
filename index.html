The challenge

Africa's greatest resource isn't beneath the ground.
It's its people.

Millions of young Africans will enter the workforce over the coming decades. Population growth alone, however, doesn't create opportunity. People need access to skills, infrastructure, networks and pathways into meaningful work.

Opportunity
Infrastructure

Without these foundations, a demographic advantage can become a massive employment and development challenge.
